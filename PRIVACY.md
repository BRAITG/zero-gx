# Zero-GX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Zero-GX does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network features. It does not connect to the internet.
- Everything the game saves stays on your device, in the game's private storage: your best scores, recordings of your runs, the levels you build in the level editor, and your settings. Android does not back this data up, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.
- The only permission the game requests is vibration, used for feedback when your ship is hit or destroyed.

If a future version adds online features such as shared levels or rankings, this policy will be updated before that version is released. It will describe what data is collected, why, and how to have it deleted.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/BRAITG/zero-gx.
