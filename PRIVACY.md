# Zero-GX Privacy Policy (draft)

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Zero-GX does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network features.
- Your best scores are saved only on your device, in the game's private storage, and are deleted when you uninstall the game.
- The only permission the game requests is vibration, used for feedback when your ship is hit.

If a future version adds online features such as cloud saves or rankings, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and it will describe what data is collected and why.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/BRAITG/zero-gx.
