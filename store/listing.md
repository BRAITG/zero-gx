**Full description** (4000 characters max):

Space has no friction. Once you are moving, you keep moving, and the only way to stop is to fire your engine the other way.

Zero-GX is a physics puzzle-racer about flying a small spaceship from start to goal through courses full of planets, black holes, stars and asteroids. Every planet pulls you in. Slingshot around them to turn for free, or burn precious fuel fighting their pull.

- Touch and hold to steer and fire your engine; let go to coast.
- Seven guided training stages teach frictionless flight, walls, fuel, gravity, black holes and stars.
- Twelve levels: bounce off blue walls, avoid orange and lethal red ones, dodge drifting asteroids, slip past black holes, and thread past stars.
- Pickups boost or cripple your engine, and a free-burning boost can save an empty tank.
- Every finish is scored on fuel left, hull left and time.

Monetization-enabled Google Play builds may show small banners on Home, between-stage results, Community and Settings. There are no ads during a flight or full-screen, rewarded or app-open ads. All levels, Training and the editor remain free, with no gameplay purchases, subscriptions or consumable currency. Purchases can be restored in Settings.
